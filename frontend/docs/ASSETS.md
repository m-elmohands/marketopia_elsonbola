## Final image prompts

Built-in image-generation tool, food still life:

> Create one photorealistic editorial food still-life photograph for an Egyptian rice brand website. Wide horizontal 3:2 composition. Top-down to slightly angled view of a beautiful shallow handmade matte ivory ceramic bowl piled with cooked white Egyptian short-grain rice, subtle fluffy individual grains visible, a few loose uncooked rice grains scattered on a warm pale limestone tabletop, a small natural linen napkin and one elegant dried golden wheat stem along the edge. Bowl placed mainly at the right center, generous quiet cream negative space on the left, warm natural afternoon sunlight from upper left, soft sophisticated shadows, premium Mediterranean food magazine photography, tactile subtle film grain, minimalist warm cream palette with tiny dark olive accent from napkin. No packaging, no logos, no text, no humans, no utensils in rice. This is an atmospheric culinary illustration, not a photograph of the client's actual product. Save generated image for use as local website hero asset.

Built-in image-editing tool, packaging composition, transparent background:

Preserve the bags' original shape, colors, sizes relative to one another, printed brand logo and Arabic/English lettering as accurately as possible. Remove EVERYTHING outside the bags: all factory scenery, title typography, bottom contact bar, rice bowl, foliage, background, scene grain, shadows outside the bags. Three bags arranged together on a single transparent canvas as in the original, fully visible, front facing. Do not redesign packaging or add claims. This is a background removal asset for the client's website. Transparent background mandatory.
